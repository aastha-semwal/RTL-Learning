# Day 19 - MOD-10 (Decade) Counter

## Objective

Design and verify a 4-bit MOD-10 Counter using Verilog HDL.

---

## Theory

A MOD-10 Counter counts from 0 to 9 and resets back to 0.

It is also known as a Decade Counter.

---

## Inputs

- clk
- reset

---

## Output

- Q[3:0]
